# Nigeria: major problems and the opportunity we picked

Research date: November 2026. Figures come from news, IMF/World Bank and law-firm sources. Several conflict with each other, and this file says where.

## The big problems

| Problem | Key facts | Can a near-zero-budget software business help? |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost of living / food inflation** | Headline inflation fell to 14.63% (Sep 2026), but **food inflation is still 19.59%**. The IMF puts poverty at **63%** (national line). | Only indirectly. Helping traders see their real profit helps them price correctly. |
| **Food loss & farm-to-market** | Estimates of food lost between harvest and market run from **30–50%** (about ₦3.5tn a year per the Agriculture Ministry). Farmers sell at distress prices. Up to 34.7m people face severe food insecurity in the 2026 lean season. | Needs logistics, cold storage and capital. Not feasible under $50. |
| **Jobs: unemployment & underemployment** | NBS headline unemployment is low (~4–5%) under the ILO method, but much of the workforce is underemployed or informal. Other surveys put youth joblessness far higher. | **Yes.** A paid agent network gives young people commission income. |
| **Power** | Unstable supply raises costs for every business. | Partly: the app is built to work offline and on low battery and data. |
| **New 2026 tax rules: small businesses unprepared** | The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 took effect in January 2026. The Tax Administration Act requires **every business, taxable or not, to keep books for 6 years**. Small businesses get big exemptions (0% tax for small companies; first ₦800k of personal income tax-free), **but they need records to prove they qualify**. ICAN and others warn that most SMEs keep weak records. | **Yes — best fit.** Urgent, affects about 40m businesses, solvable with software. |

## Why we picked "records + tax readiness" for small traders

1. **A new legal requirement makes it urgent.** Record-keeping is now mandatory, and records are how a trader proves they owe ₦0 tax.
2. **The market is huge.** There are about 40 million MSMEs, producing most of Nigeria's employment, and over 90% of them are informal.
3. **Distribution is cheap.** WhatsApp is on about 95% of Nigerian smartphones (Orange/KPMG 2026). Agents onboard traders face to face in markets.
4. **There is room on price.** Similar tools charge ₦6,500–₦10,000 a month (TaxBook AI Growth ₦9,500, Kudipod ₦9,999). Ankoo Pro costs **₦1,500/month**, which suits market traders.
5. **It creates jobs too.** Agents earn 20% commission, which tackles youth underemployment directly.

## Uncertainties you must check before claiming anything to customers

- **Small-company turnover limit:** most guides say ₦100m, but a KPMG note says the enacted text is ₦50m. The app defaults to ₦100m and reads the limit from `SMALL_COMPANY_TURNOVER_NGN`. Confirm it against the Act or with the NRS.
- **Personal income tax bands** in the app (0% to ₦800k, then 15/18/21/23/25%) are estimates. They leave out reliefs such as rent relief and pension.
- Always present results as **estimates, not tax advice**. The app and report already say this.

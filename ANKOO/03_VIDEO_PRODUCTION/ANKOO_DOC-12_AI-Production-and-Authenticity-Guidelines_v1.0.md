---

## 1. Principle

> **AI supports the real brand. It never replaces its authenticity.**

ANKOO's value depends on trust: that the garment you see is the garment you receive, that the founder's story is true, and that the craft is real. AI may speed up and enrich production, but it must never blur those truths.

---

## 2. Traffic-light policy

### 🟢 Permitted (log in AI register)
- Concept development: moodboards, look exploration, location ideas, lighting tests, storyboard frames
- Script drafting support (final words written/approved by humans; founder lines approved by the founder)
- Transcription, subtitle timing, translation **drafts** (Yoruba checked and rewritten by a fluent human)
- Technical post-production: noise reduction, stabilisation, upscaling of real footage, dialogue clean-up, rotoscoping, object removal of distractions (e.g., a cable, a sign) that does not alter the garment or meaning
- Background extension/cleanup that does not misrepresent a real location as something it is not
- Approved founder cloned-voice narration for approved scripts (see §6)
- Supplementary atmospheric shots (sky, texture, abstract light) **clearly not presented as ANKOO documentation**

### 🟡 Permitted with founder approval and clear labelling
- AI-generated scenes used as **conceptual or stylised storytelling** (e.g., an abstract dream sequence) where no reasonable viewer would take them as documentation
- AI-generated "concept garments" for internal design exploration, never shown publicly as available products
- Synthetic brand narration voice (not the founder) for product campaigns

### 🔴 Never permitted
- Presenting fictional garments as real ANKOO products
- Altering garment construction, colour, fabric pattern, embroidery, fit or details in a way that misrepresents the product
- AI-generated customers, testimonials, reviews or "real people" wearing ANKOO
- AI-generated factories, workshops, artisans or production scenes presented as genuine ANKOO process
- AI-generated or altered founder imagery, likeness or experiences presented as real
- Using the founder's cloned voice for words he has not approved
- **Recreating, regenerating or approximating the ANKOO logo with AI** (DOC-03)
- Generating images of identifiable real people without consent
- AI generation imitating specific living artists, photographers or other brands' campaigns
- AI-generated "traditional" symbols, patterns or text with unverified cultural meaning

---

## 3. Product truth test

Before any AI process touches footage containing a garment, ask:

1. Would a customer receiving this garment see exactly what this frame shows?
2. Are colour, pattern, embroidery, construction and proportions unchanged?
3. Would we be comfortable showing the original and processed frames side by side to the customer?

If any answer is no, do not use the processed frame.

---

## 4. Disclosure

- Internally, **every** AI use is logged (§7).
- Publicly, where AI-generated imagery is used in a way a viewer might take as real, disclose it (e.g., "Concept imagery created with AI") and comply with each platform's AI-content labelling rules (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube all have them).
- Founder cloned voice: follow platform rules on synthetic media and disclose where required.

---

## 5. Data and asset safety

- Upload only the assets needed to AI tools; prefer tools whose terms do not claim training rights over uploaded content.
- Never upload unreleased collection designs to tools that may train on or publish them without founder approval.
- Never upload the official logo master files to generative tools.
- Keep AI outputs in the campaign's `11_AI_REGISTER/` folder, separate from source footage.

---

## 6. Founder voice and likeness

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Real voice | Preferred for genuine founder communication and personal appearances |
| Cloned voice | Permitted only with **documented consent from Bolu Felix** (consent record stored in `07_FOUNDER_STORY/VOICE/`), for scripts he has approved line by line |
| Cloned voice in Yoruba | Only if a fluent reviewer confirms pronunciation and tone are natural; otherwise record real voice |
| Visual likeness (AI avatar, face generation) | Not permitted without separate explicit written approval for each use |
| Revocation | The founder may withdraw consent; cloned-voice content is then retired |

Voice clone source files and access credentials are restricted to named people.

---

## 8. Cultural accuracy and AI

AI tools often produce inaccurate or stereotyped depictions of Yoruba and Nigerian dress, textiles and ceremonies, and unreliable Yoruba language. Therefore:
- AI cultural visuals are for internal mood only unless reviewed and approved.
- AI Yoruba translations are drafts only; a fluent human writes the final.
- Never use AI-generated pattern or symbol designs as "traditional" without verification.

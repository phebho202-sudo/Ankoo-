## 1. The fifteen-stage ANKOO production workflow

Each stage has an output and a gate. Do not move to the next stage until the gate is passed.

| # | Stage | Output | Gate (who signs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Receive campaign brief** | Completed brief (DOC-07 §5) | Creative lead confirms brief is complete |
| 2 | **Identify campaign objective** | One-line objective + success measure | Founder agrees objective |
| 3 | **Review footage and brand assets** | Asset audit (§3 below) listing what exists and what must be captured | Creative lead |
| 4 | **Establish creative concept** | Concept statement (≤150 words) naming The Moment, The Unity and the signatures used | Founder |
| 5 | **Visual references and storyboard** | Moodboard (real references, AI concept frames labelled per DOC-12) + storyboard | Founder |
| 6 | **Develop shot sequence** | Shot list including Garment Coverage Standard (DOC-04 §4) | Creative lead + DOP |
| 7 | **Prepare narration and script** | Script (EN/YO); founder lines approved in writing | Founder (mandatory for founder voice) |
| 8 | **Establish sound direction** | Music brief / licensed track shortlist / licence register | Creative lead |
| 9 | **Edit the master film** | Assembly → rough → fine cut → picture lock (DOC-06 §5) | Founder at picture lock |
| 10 | **Colour grade** | Graded master (DOC-02 §4) | Colourist + creative lead |
| 11 | **Add typography and official logo** | Titles, captions, end card using the official logo only (DOC-03) | Creative lead |
| 12 | **Create platform-specific versions** | All deliverables per DOC-09 matrix, recomposed, not blind-cropped | Editor |
| 13 | **Visual and audio QC** | Completed QC checklist (DOC-10) for every file | QC reviewer |
| 14 | **Prepare final publishing assets** | Captions (.srt), post copy, thumbnails/covers, hashtags, alt text, schedule | Social lead |
| 15 | **Present for approval** | Approval pack: master, versions, QC sheets, AI register, licences | **Founder — explicit written approval** |

> **Nothing is published or distributed without explicit approval.**
> **Original footage and source assets are never overwritten.**

## 4. Source protection protocol

1. On the shoot, back up each card to **two** drives with checksum verification before formatting.
2. Ingest to `02_SOURCE_READ_ONLY/` using the original camera folder structure. **Never rename original camera files.**
3. Set folder permissions to read-only after ingest; store a third copy off-site or in cloud storage.
4. Work only on proxies, copies or linked media.
5. Edits, grades and exports are always new files with new version numbers.
6. Brand assets (logos, fonts) are copied from `06_BRAND_ASSETS/…/00_ORIGINALS_READ_ONLY/` into project folders; originals are never opened for editing.
7. Nothing is deleted from source or approved folders. Superseded versions move to `_archive/`.

## 7. Approval pack (Stage 15)

Present to the founder in one folder: `05_CAMPAIGNS/<campaign>/08_EXPORTS_REVIEW/APPROVAL_PACK_v<NN>/`

- [ ] Master film (watermarked review copy)
- [ ] All platform versions (watermarked)
- [ ] Captions and post copy for each platform
- [ ] Thumbnails / covers
- [ ] Completed QC checklists (DOC-10)
- [ ] Music licence register
- [ ] AI usage register (DOC-12)
- [ ] Talent and location releases confirmation
- [ ] Yoruba language review sign-off (if applicable)
- [ ] Approval form:

```
I approve the following files for publication on the platforms listed:
Files: ___________________________________
Platforms: _______________________________
Conditions / changes required: ____________
Name: Bolu Felix        Date: __________     Signature / written confirmation: __________
```

After approval, unwatermarked finals are exported, named with `_APPROVED`, and **copied** into `10_FINAL_APPROVED_CONTENT/<YYYY>/<campaign>/` and `09_SOCIAL_MEDIA_EXPORTS/<platform>/`.

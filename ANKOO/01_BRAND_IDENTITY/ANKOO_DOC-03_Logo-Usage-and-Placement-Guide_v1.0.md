## 1. Policy

ANKOO uses **only its original, official logo**, taken from the master brand asset folder. The logo is the brand's signature; it must look identical everywhere it appears.

### The eight rules
1. **Never recreate the logo using AI**, tracing, retyping or redrawing.
2. **Never distort its proportions.** Scale only with the aspect ratio locked.
3. **Never change its typography**, letter spacing or weight.
4. **Never introduce an alternative logo**, monogram or wordmark that has not been approved.
5. **Never use an unapproved variation** (colour, outline, effect, cropped or partial versions).
6. **Maintain clear space** on all sides (§4).
7. **Use approved brand colours only** (§5, DOC-02).
8. **Keep placement subtle and intentional.** The logo closes a statement; it does not decorate every frame.

## 3. Where the logo appears

| Placement | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening campaign frame** | Optional, brief | A small, quiet mark on black or a held image for 1.5–2 s. Many ANKOO films open on image alone and save the logo for the close; this is preferred for brand films. |
| **Closing frame / end card** | **Always** | The primary logo position. See §6. |
| **Brand documentary end card** | Always | Logo + tagline. |
| **Product campaign videos** | Close; optional open | |
| **Social campaign graphics** | Once per graphic | Small, consistent corner or centred footer. |
| **Website campaign visuals** | Where layout requires | The site header usually carries the logo; do not duplicate it inside hero films. |

### Where the logo does not appear
- Not as a persistent watermark across a whole film.
- Not on every scene or every slide of a carousel (once per carousel, on the first or last card).
- Not over garment details, faces or embroidery.
- Not animated with spins, glows, shine sweeps, 3D extrusions, particle effects or bounce.

---

## 4. Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep an empty margin on every side equal to **at least the height of the logo's capital letter "A"** (or, if the logo has a symbol, half the symbol's height). Once the official file arrives, define the exact unit from it and update this section.
- **Minimum size (video):** logo width not less than **12%** of frame width on 16:9; **28%** of frame width on 9:16.
- **Maximum size (end card):** logo width not more than **35%** of frame width on 16:9; **55%** on 9:16. ANKOO is quiet; bigger is not better.
- **Minimum size (print/packaging):** to be set once the vector master is supplied.

---

## 5. Colour versions

Use only colour versions that exist in the official files. Expected combinations (confirm against the files):

| Background | Logo version |
|---|---|
| ANKOO Black / dark image | Ivory (reversed) |
| ANKOO Forest | Ivory (reversed) |
| ANKOO Ivory / cream / light image | Black |

Do not place the logo on busy image areas. If the image behind is busy, choose a calmer frame; do not add boxes, blur panels or drop shadows behind the logo.

## 6. The ANKOO end card standard

```
[ near-silence: the Still Beat, 1–2 s, DOC-01 Signature 6 ]
[ fade from final image to clean field: Black, Forest or held dark image, 12–18 frames ]
[ LOGO — centred, optical centre slightly above geometric centre ]
[ tagline appears 0.5–1 s later, below logo, small: THE MOMENT THAT DEFINES A MAN. ]
[ hold 2.5–4 s total ]
[ optional: website or handle in small sans-serif, bottom third, only for social/ads ]
[ fade to black ]
```

- Logo animation: a single slow fade-in (12–24 frames). Nothing else.
- Tagline typography: per DOC-01 §7, wide tracking, ivory on dark.
- Sound: a single sustained tone, a breath of ambience, or silence. No logo "sting" with whooshes.

### Social end card (9:16)
Same structure, logo centred vertically within the safe zone (DOC-09), total duration 2–3 s.

---

## 7. Watermarks and handles

- No watermark on campaign films.
- For reposted or shareable short clips, a small handle (text, not logo) in the bottom safe zone is acceptable when required by the social strategy.

---

## 8. Misuse examples (do not do)

- AI-generated "ANKOO" lettering in any image or video.
- Stretched or squashed logo to fit a frame.
- Logo recoloured gold, metallic or gradient.
- Logo outlined, embossed, bevelled or given a shadow.
- Logo placed over the garment or a face.
- Logo cropped by the frame edge or inside platform UI areas.
- Logo combined with other marks, emojis or stickers.
- Any version from screenshots, social media downloads or low-resolution copies.

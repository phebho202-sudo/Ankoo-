## 2. Truth check: what this script is based on

Every statement in the narration comes from the facts you provided on 10 Nov 2026:
1. **What you saw:** an opportunity in Nigeria's traditional fashion and event industry. Nigerians dress beautifully for weddings, introductions, birthdays and important occasions, and matching outfits are a major part of the culture. There was room to improve how traditional fashion is designed, produced, presented and positioned. You wanted to help change that perception by bringing sophistication, structure, creativity and a refined luxury experience.
2. **The name:** from the expression *"E ja se Ankoo"*: coming together in matching outfits; unity, togetherness, identity, belonging. You wanted a brand identity that carries that meaning. **No specific family occasion or personal incident is claimed.**
3. **The vision:** bigger than making traditional clothes. Elevate Nigerian traditional fashion, celebrate cultural identity, present it with the sophistication it deserves, and become a recognised contemporary African luxury menswear and lifestyle brand.

Nothing else is claimed: no dates, no events, no workshops, no customers, no achievements.

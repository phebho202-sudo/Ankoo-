# Campaign 01 — ANKOO: THE MOMENT

**Status:** v01 — Recommended first campaign, awaiting founder approval and assets
**Pillar:** A. The Brand Film (with G. Social Campaign delivery)
**Campaign code:** `MOMENT`

---

## Why this campaign first

- It establishes all six ANKOO signatures (DOC-01 §4) in one film, creating the reference every later production will follow.
- It needs **no invented founder facts**: it dramatises the tagline and the meaning of *E ja se Ankoo*, using real garments.
- It produces the master end card, sonic signature, grade and typography system that later campaigns reuse.
- It provides the hero content for Phase 2 of the 90-day plan (DOC-11) and the website homepage film.

---

## Objective

Introduce ANKOO's Quiet Luxury identity and make the tagline felt: **THE MOMENT THAT DEFINES A MAN.**

Success measure: completion rate and saves/shares above the Week-1 baseline; qualitative audience response describing the brand as refined, cultural and premium.

## Concept (≤150 words)

A man prepares for an occasion that matters. We never learn exactly what it is. We see the care: fabric unfolded, a sleeve settled, a cap placed. He pauses at a threshold, light beyond the doorway, and steps through. On the other side, other men stand dressed in coordinated ANKOO: brothers, friends, family. Nobody speaks. They close the distance between them. The final image is the Gathering: symmetrical, still, dignified. Sound falls away. The ANKOO logo. *The moment that defines a man.*

- **The Moment:** the step through the threshold.
- **The Unity:** the Gathering in matching ANKOO (*E ja se Ankoo*).

## Structure (master 60–90 s)

| Act | Time | Content | Signatures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Stillness | 0–8 s | Black → Fabric Close of the hero textile; room tone | Fabric Close, Held Frame |
| 2. Preparation | 8–30 s | Hands, sleeve, collar, cap, mirror; wide–medium–close rhythm | Held Frame, Forest & Ivory |
| 3. The Threshold | 30–45 s | Man in doorway, light beyond; slow push-in; he steps through | Threshold |
| 4. The Gathering | 45–70 s | Men in coordinated ANKOO; slow lateral track; final symmetrical portrait | Gathering |
| 5. Close | 70–80 s | Still Beat → logo → tagline | Still Beat |

Narration: optional, maximum three lines, founder's real voice (or approved clone) — lines to be written with and approved by Bolu Felix. A no-narration version is also delivered.

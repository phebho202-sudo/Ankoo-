# ANKOO — Creative Production System

**ANKOO — THE MOMENT THAT DEFINES A MAN.**
Founder: Bolu Felix · Creative direction: **Quiet Luxury** (permanent) · Issued 2026-11-09 · v1.0, awaiting founder approval

This folder is ANKOO's permanent reference for cinematic campaign production. Start with **DOC-01**; it governs the rest.

> This repository also contains a separate web app called "Ankoo" (a bookkeeping app for traders, in `src/` and `public/`). The `ANKOO/` folder is independent of that app and does not change it. The app's green icon is **not** the fashion brand logo.

---

## 1. The direction in summary

ANKOO is a contemporary Nigerian luxury menswear and lifestyle brand built on Yoruba textile heritage and the idea of *E ja se Ankoo*: unity through matching dress. On screen it communicates luxury through **restraint, craftsmanship, composition, texture and atmosphere**, never through loud effects.

Six signatures make ANKOO recognisable before the logo appears:

1. **The Held Frame** — stillness; shots held longer than social media expects.
2. **The Threshold** — a man framed in architecture at the instant before he steps into his moment.
3. **The Fabric Close** — macro study of real ANKOO textile in raking light.
4. **Forest and Ivory** — forest green, deep black, warm ivory, built on set, never tinted onto the garment.
5. **The Gathering** — men together in coordinated ANKOO, composed symmetrically: the brand's name made visible.
6. **The Still Beat** — near-silence before a quiet logo end card.

The garment is always the hero and always true to the product. Founder stories are always real. AI assists but never fakes.

## 5. Recommended first campaign: **ANKOO — THE MOMENT**

A 60–90 second brand film. A man prepares for an occasion that matters, pauses at a threshold, steps through, and joins other men in coordinated ANKOO: the Gathering. Then the Still Beat, the logo and the tagline. It uses all six signatures, needs no invented founder facts, and creates the end card, grade, typography and sound system that every later campaign reuses. Full brief: `05_CAMPAIGNS/ANKOO_2026_MOMENT/00_BRIEF/`.

## 6. Production checklist: THE MOMENT

**Pre-production**
- [ ] Founder approves DOC-01 and the campaign concept
- [ ] Official logo files placed in `06_BRAND_ASSETS/LOGO/00_ORIGINALS_READ_ONLY/`; end-card template built
- [ ] Palette and fonts confirmed (DOC-02, DOC-01 §7)
- [ ] Hero look + 3–5 coordinated looks selected (real ANKOO garments), fitted and pressed
- [ ] Garment reference stills shot under daylight with a colour chart
- [ ] Location with an architectural threshold and a calm space for the Gathering; permission secured
- [ ] Talent cast; release forms signed
- [ ] Director, DOP, gaffer, stylist, sound recordist confirmed
- [ ] Storyboard and shot list, including the Garment Coverage Standard G1–G14 (DOC-04 §4) and vertical setups
- [ ] Narration lines (if any) written and approved by the founder; Yoruba version by a fluent writer
- [ ] Music: original motif commissioned or track licensed; licence filed

**Shoot**
- [ ] Colour chart and grey card per setup; log, 10-bit, 4K, 24 fps (48–60 fps for fabric)
- [ ] Threshold, Fabric Close, Held Frames and Gathering captured, horizontal and vertical
- [ ] Room tone and foley (fabric, footsteps, cap) recorded
- [ ] Media double-backed-up with checksums before cards are reused

**Post-production**
- [ ] Ingest to `02_SOURCE_READ_ONLY` and lock it
- [ ] Assembly → rough → fine cut → founder picture-lock approval
- [ ] Grade per DOC-02 (skin and garment truth checked on monitor and phone)
- [ ] Mix to −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP; stems exported
- [ ] Titles, captions, official logo end card per DOC-03
- [ ] Cutdowns and platform versions recomposed per DOC-09
- [ ] QC sheet completed for every file (DOC-10); AI register completed (DOC-12)

**Approval and release**
- [ ] Approval pack assembled (DOC-08 §7)
- [ ] **Explicit written approval from Bolu Felix**
- [ ] Approved finals copied to `10_FINAL_APPROVED_CONTENT/` and `09_SOCIAL_MEDIA_EXPORTS/`
- [ ] Published per DOC-11 Phase 2 schedule; Week-1 baseline recorded for comparison

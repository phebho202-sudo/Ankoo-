## 1. Purpose

This is the governing creative reference for ANKOO's moving image. It defines what ANKOO looks, sounds and feels like on screen, so that every production, by any director, editor or agency, reads as one brand.

The aim is not to make videos that look expensive. **The aim is a visual identity people recognise before they see the logo.**

---

## 2. Brand foundation (as given, do not embellish)

- ANKOO is a contemporary Nigerian luxury menswear and lifestyle brand.
- It is rooted in African cultural heritage, Yoruba textile traditions, Nigerian traditional fashion and the importance of unity.
- The name comes from the Nigerian expression **"E ja se Ankoo"**: wearing matching outfits, coming together, expressing unity through clothing.
- ANKOO believes traditional African fashion deserves a more sophisticated presentation, stronger production standards and a place in contemporary luxury fashion.
- It elevates traditional and event fashion through refined construction, contemporary African silhouettes, exceptional fabric selection, authentic cultural references, sophisticated styling, luxury presentation and packaging, strong visual storytelling and a consistent customer experience.
- **Tagline:** THE MOMENT THAT DEFINES A MAN.
- **Founder:** Bolu Felix. His genuine journey, vision and experiences are part of ANKOO's storytelling identity.

**Truth rule:** No team member, agency or AI tool may invent founder experiences, customer testimonials, production facilities, historical claims or brand achievements. Anything not documented in `07_FOUNDER_STORY/` or confirmed in writing by the founder does not go on screen.

## 3. The creative philosophy: Quiet Luxury

ANKOO communicates luxury through **restraint, confidence, craftsmanship, composition, texture and atmosphere**. It never tries to look luxurious; the quality of the presentation does that work.

### 3.1 What ANKOO is on screen

Timeless · Refined · Cinematic · Masculine · Understated · Sophisticated · Architectural · Cultural · Emotionally engaging · Internationally competitive

### 3.2 What ANKOO never is

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| Loud, fast, trend-chasing edits | Held frames, deliberate rhythm |
| Flash transitions, glitches, whooshes, spins | Straight cuts, slow dissolves, match cuts |
| Oversaturated, "Instagram filter" colour | Controlled saturation, true fabric colour |
| Gold-foil graphics, shine sweeps, 3D text | Quiet typography with generous space |
| Generic AI fashion faces and fantasy sets | Real garments, real people, real places |
| Stock "motivational" music | Minimal, atmospheric, contemporary African sound |
| Logo on every frame | Logo at the opening and the close, placed with intent |

### 3.3 The three tests every frame must pass

1. **Is the garment the hero?** If the eye goes to an effect, a prop or a location first, recompose.
2. **Is it true?** Colour, cut and detail must match the product the customer will receive.
3. **Is it calm?** If the frame or cut is trying to grab attention, slow it down.

## 4. The ANKOO signatures: how people recognise the brand without the logo

These six devices appear, in some form, in every ANKOO production. They are the brand's visual fingerprint. Use at least **four** in any film over 30 seconds and at least **two** in any social edit.

### Signature 1 — The Held Frame
ANKOO holds shots longer than social media expects: a beat of stillness before and after movement. A man standing still in a well-cut garment is the strongest image the brand has. Minimum hero-shot duration: 2.5 seconds in social edits, 4 seconds in films.

### Signature 2 — The Threshold
The tagline is about *the moment*. ANKOO visualises it as a threshold: a man framed in a doorway, an arch, a corridor or a gate, at the instant before he steps into an occasion. Architecture frames the man; the man frames the moment. Every campaign contains at least one threshold shot.

### Signature 3 — The Fabric Close
A macro study of real textile: the weave of aso-oke, the stitch line of embroidery, the fold of an agbada sleeve, the edge of a cap. Lit from the side so texture casts its own shadow. Every film contains at least one Fabric Close, and it must be the actual ANKOO fabric.

### Signature 4 — Forest and Ivory
The colour world is forest green, deep black and warm ivory, carried by locations, set dressing, light and grade, never by tinting the garment. See DOC-02.

### Signature 5 — The Gathering (Ankoo)
The brand's name is unity. Its signature closing image is **men together in coordinated ANKOO garments**: composed symmetrically, unhurried, dignified. Not a party shot; a portrait of togetherness. Used in brand films, collection campaigns and event content.

### Signature 6 — The Still Beat
Before the logo end card, sound drops to near-silence (room tone, a breath, a single sustained note) for 1–2 seconds. Then the logo arrives on a clean field. This is ANKOO's sonic and visual full stop.

## 6. Voice and narration

- **Bolu Felix's real voice** for genuine founder communication and personal appearances.
- **Bolu Felix's approved cloned voice** for narration where appropriate, only with documented consent on file (see DOC-12 §6) and only for words he has approved.
- **A separate brand narration voice** may be developed for product campaigns; it must not be presented as the founder.

Voice direction: calm, confident, natural, mature, reflective, authentic, unhurried. Never a generic advert narrator. Short sentences. Space between lines.

**Yoruba:** natural conversational Yoruba, correct tone marks in all on-screen text, pronunciation checked by a fluent Yoruba speaker before release. English and Yoruba versions are separate masters, not subtitles of each other.

## 7. Typography direction (provisional until brand fonts are supplied)

ANKOO's brand typeface has not been supplied (see §10). Until it is:

- Use **one** refined serif for titles and **one** clean sans-serif for captions and information. Never more than two families in a piece.
- Titles: uppercase with wide letter-spacing (+100 to +200 tracking), small relative to frame.
- Captions: sentence case, medium weight, high legibility.
- Colour: warm ivory on dark images, deep black on light images. No outlines, drop shadows, gradients or animated text effects beyond a slow fade.
- Yoruba text must render tone marks correctly. Test the font with Yoruba characters and tone marks (ẹ, ọ, ṣ, á, à, é, è, ọ́, ẹ̀) before adopting it.

> Note: the exact Yoruba spelling and tone marking of the brand expression should be confirmed by the founder. This brief uses "E ja se Ankoo" as supplied.

## 8. Authenticity commitments

1. Real ANKOO garments, footage, founder material and assets first.
2. Garment colour, cut, pattern and finish are never altered in a way that misrepresents the product.
3. AI-generated material is labelled internally, used only for concept, mood or clearly supplementary storytelling, and never presented as documentation of ANKOO garments, customers, workshops or the founder (DOC-12).
4. Cultural references are researched and checked with knowledgeable Yoruba sources before release.
5. Nothing is published or distributed without explicit approval.
6. Original footage and source assets are never overwritten (DOC-08 §4).

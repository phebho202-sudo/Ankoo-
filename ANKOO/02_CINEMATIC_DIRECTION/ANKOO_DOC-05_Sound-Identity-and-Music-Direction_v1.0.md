## 1. Principle

ANKOO sounds the way it looks: **atmospheric, minimal, emotional, sophisticated, contemporary African, restrained and memorable.**

Sound is half of the luxury experience. Silence and space are part of the score. Music never competes with narration, and it never tells the audience how to feel at full volume.

---

## 2. The ANKOO sound palette

| Element | Character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Subtle African percussion** | Talking drum (dùndún) used sparingly as a voice rather than a beat; shekere, udu, soft hand percussion | One or two textures at a time, often distant or low in the mix. Never a dense drum groove. |
| **Instrumental textures** | Kora-like or guitar plucks, kalimba/agidigbo-style tones, low strings, solo cello, felt piano, soft brass swells | Each instrument earns its place. |
| **Cinematic ambience** | Room tone, wind through a courtyard, distant city, fabric rustle, footsteps on stone | Recorded on location wherever possible. |
| **Understated orchestral** | Sustained strings, low drones, a single horn line | For brand films and cultural stories. |
| **Voice as instrument** | Hummed or wordless vocal, call-and-response fragments | Must be original, commissioned or properly licensed; any Yoruba lyrics checked for meaning. |
| **Foley** | Fabric movement, cap placed on a table, footsteps, the click of a box, the turn of a page | ANKOO's signature detail sounds. Clean, close, real. |

### Avoid
- Generic "inspirational" or corporate motivational tracks
- Trap, EDM drops, heavy commercial Afrobeats production used as wallpaper
- Excessive drums or busy percussion
- Whooshes, risers and impacts on transitions
- Trending viral audio that contradicts the brand tone (see §6 for social)
- Music that competes with narration

## 3. The ANKOO sonic signature

To make ANKOO recognisable by ear, develop a short **sonic signature**: a 2–4 second motif used consistently at the end card (DOC-03 §6).

**Brief for the composer:**
- One or two instruments only (for example, a low talking-drum phrase answered by a sustained string or felt-piano note).
- Emotionally warm, dignified, unhurried.
- Works at very low volume and on phone speakers.
- Exists in three versions: full (4 s), short (2 s) and a single sustained tone for the Still Beat.
- Commissioned as original work with ANKOO owning or holding a perpetual, worldwide, all-media licence.

Until the signature is commissioned, end cards use near-silence with room tone.

## 4. Direction by content type

| Content | Music | Sound design | Narration relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brand film** | Original or premium-licensed score; slow build; orchestral + African textures; climax restrained | Architectural ambience, footsteps, fabric | Music ducks fully under narration; swells only in gaps |
| **Founder documentary** | Sparse; often no music under interviews; light themes between chapters | Real room tone and location sound dominant | Founder voice is the lead instrument |
| **Collection campaign** | Rhythmic but minimal; a pulse rather than a beat; tempo ~60–90 BPM | Fabric, footsteps, garment foley foregrounded | Little or no narration; music leads |
| **Craftsmanship film** | Very sparse; often none | Workshop sounds: scissors, machines, chalk, iron, hands on cloth | Optional founder or artisan voice |
| **Cultural story** | Traditional instrumentation treated respectfully; authenticity checked | Natural environments | Narration in Yoruba and/or English |
| **Product films** | Short motif-based cues | Close foley of the product | Brand narrator or none |
| **Social edits** | Same family, shorter cues; may open with sound or silence-to-sound for attention | Foley carries the hook | Captions mandatory (most watch muted) |

## 5. Licensing and rights

1. Use **only licensed, original or otherwise properly authorised music**.
2. Keep a **music licence register** in each campaign folder: track, composer, publisher, licence type, territory, term, media, platforms, proof of licence.
3. Production music libraries must permit **advertising/commercial use** across all ANKOO platforms (including paid social and website).
4. Commissioned music: written agreement assigning ownership or a perpetual licence; stems delivered.
5. Traditional or folk melodies: confirm they are in the public domain or cleared, and credit performers.
6. Platform-library sounds (e.g., TikTok or Instagram libraries) may be used **only within that platform's terms** and never in paid ads unless the licence covers commercial use.
7. No uncleared commercial tracks, ever, even on Stories or WhatsApp Status.

---

## 6. Social sound

- Every social edit must work **with sound off** (captions, visual storytelling) and **with sound on** (sound design rewards attention).
- Open with an intriguing sound (fabric movement, a single drum note, a footstep) rather than loud music.
- Trending audio is optional and only when it fits the brand tone and its licence allows the use.

## 7. Mixing and mastering standard

| Deliverable | Integrated loudness | True peak | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp) | −14 LUFS | −1.0 dBTP | Check on phone speakers and earbuds |
| YouTube | −14 LUFS | −1.0 dBTP | |
| Website (homepage loops) | Usually muted; if audio enabled, −16 to −18 LUFS | −1.0 dBTP | Provide a mute-first experience |
| Broadcast / cinema (if ever required) | Per broadcaster spec (e.g., −23 LUFS EBU R128) | Per spec | |

**Mix priorities:**
1. Narration clear and intimate. Dialogue sits around −20 to −16 LUFS short-term; music ducked 8–12 dB beneath it.
2. Foley and ambience give space and texture.
3. Music supports, rarely leads, except in collection films.
4. Stereo master; 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV for masters; AAC 320 kbps for platform exports.

**Stems to archive per film:** dialogue/narration, music, sound effects/foley, ambience, full mix, music-and-effects (M&E) for alternate-language versions.

---

## 8. Sound checklist

- [ ] All music licensed or original; licence register complete
- [ ] Sonic signature (or Still Beat silence) at end card
- [ ] Narration intelligible over music on phone speakers
- [ ] No whooshes, risers or impacts on transitions
- [ ] Loudness and true peak within spec
- [ ] M&E stem exported for Yoruba/English versions
- [ ] Room tone used to smooth edits; no dead digital silence unless intended

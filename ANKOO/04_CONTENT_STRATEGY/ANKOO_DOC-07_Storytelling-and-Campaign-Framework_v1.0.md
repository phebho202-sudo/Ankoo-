## 1. The ANKOO story in one line

> **ANKOO dresses men for the moments that define them, and brings them together in those moments.**

Every story ANKOO tells connects to one or both of these ideas:
1. **The Moment** — the occasion, the threshold, the instant a man steps into who he is (from the tagline).
2. **The Unity** — coming together, matching, belonging (from *E ja se Ankoo*).

---

## 2. Storytelling rules

1. **Truth first.** Founder experiences, customers, workshops, history and achievements appear only if documented and approved. If in doubt, leave it out.
2. **Show, then say.** Picture carries the story; narration is sparse.
3. **Specific beats general.** One real detail (a stitch, a hand, a fitting) is worth more than an adjective.
4. **Respect culture.** Yoruba and Nigerian traditions are presented with accuracy and dignity, checked by knowledgeable reviewers. No exoticising, no costume clichés.
5. **The garment is the hero; the man is the protagonist.**
6. **Restraint in words.** ANKOO does not say "luxury", "premium" or "best" on screen. It shows it.

## 3. The seven campaign pillars

### A. The Brand Film
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Introduce ANKOO's identity, philosophy, heritage, ambition and creative vision |
| **Narrative arc** | Silence → Threshold → Preparation (details, dressing) → The Moment (stepping into the occasion) → The Gathering (unity) → Still Beat → Logo + tagline |
| **Length** | Master 60–120 s; cutdowns 30 s, 15 s, 6 s |
| **Voice** | Founder (real or approved clone) or none; sparse lines |
| **Must include** | Signatures 1–6 (DOC-01 §4) |
| **Cadence** | One flagship per year; refreshed with each major collection |

### B. The Founder's Story
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Tell Bolu Felix's genuine journey: early passion for fashion, Phebstyle, the creation of ANKOO, the experience of building the brand |
| **Format** | Documentary chapters (3–8 min each on YouTube) + short founder reflections (30–60 s) for social |
| **Source material** | Real interviews with the founder, real archive photos/videos, real places. **No dramatised reconstructions presented as real.** If a reconstruction is used, it is clearly stylised and labelled as such. |
| **Before production** | A founder story interview session to capture facts, dates and anecdotes in his own words (see §6). |
| **Voice** | Bolu Felix's real voice |

### C. The Collection Campaign
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Present each collection as a luxury fashion editorial |
| **Focus** | Silhouette, construction, fabric, styling, movement, atmosphere |
| **Format** | Collection film (60–90 s), look films (10–20 s per look), editorial stills, detail series |
| **Narration** | Minimal or none; music-led |
| **Must include** | Garment Coverage Standard for every featured look (DOC-04 §4) |

### D. The Craftsmanship Film
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Show the real process: sketches, fabric selection, cutting, sewing, embroidery, fittings, finishing |
| **Rule** | **Only real ANKOO processes and people, filmed with consent.** If certain stages are not available on footage, they are not shown, not simulated. |
| **Format** | 60–180 s film; social series "From Cloth to Moment" (15–30 s per stage) |
| **Voice** | Founder and/or artisans in their own words (with consent) |

### E. The Cultural Story
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Communicate the heritage, meaning and inspiration behind ANKOO's designs |
| **Topics (verify each before production)** | The meaning of *E ja se Ankoo*; aso-ebi and coordinated dressing at occasions; Yoruba textiles such as aso-oke; the cap (fila) and its styles; agbada and its presence; occasions where men dress together |
| **Rule** | Each cultural claim is researched and verified with knowledgeable Yoruba sources before scripting. Credit cultural contributors. |
| **Format** | 60–120 s films; carousel explainers; Yoruba and English versions |

### F. The ANKOO Experience
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Show packaging, presentation, customer experience and events, using genuine material |
| **Rule** | Real packaging, real customers (with written consent), real events. No staged "customers" presented as real; no invented testimonials. |
| **Format** | Unboxing still-life films (20–40 s), event films, fitting experience |

### G. The Social Campaign
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Turn campaign footage into platform-specific edits without compromising identity |
| **Rule** | Every social edit is derived from an approved master or approved footage, recomposed for the platform (DOC-09), and passes QC (DOC-10). |
| **Formats** | Reels/TikToks, Stories, carousels, Shorts, WhatsApp Status, website loops |

## 4. Campaign architecture: one master, many expressions

```
                 CAMPAIGN MASTER FILM (16:9, 4K)
                            │
   ┌──────────────┬─────────┼──────────┬───────────────┐
 Cutdowns     Look/detail  Founder    Stills &       Website
 30/15/6 s    films        reflection carousels      loops
   │              │           │          │              │
 Reels · TikTok · Shorts · Stories · Facebook · WhatsApp Status · YouTube
```

Each campaign produces:
- 1 master film
- 3 cutdowns (30 s, 15 s, 6 s) in 16:9 and 9:16
- 4–8 vertical social edits (detail, look, founder, Gathering)
- 1 carousel set (4:5)
- 1 website hero loop (silent)
- Stills selection
- English and, where planned, Yoruba versions

## 6. Founder story capture (prerequisite for Pillar B)

Before any founder documentary is scripted, record a long-form interview with Bolu Felix covering only what he chooses to share. Suggested prompts:

1. When did you first notice your love for clothing? What do you remember?
2. What is Phebstyle, when did it begin, and what did it teach you?
3. What made you start ANKOO, and why this name?
4. What does *E ja se Ankoo* mean to you personally?
5. What is a moment that defines a man, in your own experience?
6. What has building ANKOO been like? What has been hardest?
7. What do you want an ANKOO customer to feel?
8. What do you want ANKOO to mean in ten years?

Transcribe, have the founder confirm facts and dates, and store in `07_FOUNDER_STORY/`. Only confirmed material is used in scripts.

---

## 7. Narration writing guide

- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Write for the ear: natural rhythm, unhurried.
- First person for founder; second or third person, sparing, for brand voice.
- No clichés ("elevate your style", "king", "drip", "level up").
- Leave silences in the script, marked as `[pause]` or `[picture only]`.
- Every founder line is approved by the founder before recording or cloning.
- Yoruba scripts are written (not machine-translated) by a fluent writer and reviewed for natural, conversational tone.

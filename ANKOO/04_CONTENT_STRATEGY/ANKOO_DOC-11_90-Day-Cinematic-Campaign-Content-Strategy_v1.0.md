## 1. Objective

Over 90 days, **establish ANKOO's Quiet Luxury cinematic identity in public** so that the audience starts to recognise ANKOO by its look, rhythm and sound, and so that the brand gains a library of real, reusable campaign assets.

### What success looks like at Day 90
1. One flagship brand film and its full family of cutdowns published.
2. One collection campaign published under the new direction.
3. Founder story introduced through genuine material.
4. A craftsmanship and a cultural story published (as far as genuine footage allows).
5. A consistent social grid and channel look across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp and the website.
6. Measured improvement against the baseline taken in Week 1 (§7).

---

## 2. Timing note

Day 1 should be the day the **official logo and garment assets are received** (see the asset gaps in `ANKOO/README.md`). If Day 1 falls in mid-to-late November 2026, Phase 2 lands in December, a peak period for weddings, ceremonies and gatherings in Nigeria. That suits *The Moment* and *The Unity* well, but **production of the brand film must be completed before the launch date, not during it.** Do not compromise quality to hit a seasonal date; slip the date instead.

## 3. The four phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation and capture (Days 1–14)
**Goal:** prepare, capture, and quietly reset the channels.

| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Assets | Receive logo, fonts, garment list; lock DOC-02 palette; build end-card template |
| Founder | Founder story interview (DOC-07 §6); voice consent record if cloned voice is to be used |
| Production | Pre-production for Campaign 01 *THE MOMENT* (see `05_CAMPAIGNS/ANKOO_2026_MOMENT/`); location scouting; casting; music brief |
| Shoot | Shoot Campaign 01 (brand film + vertical setups + stills + craft footage if available) |
| Channels | Audit existing posts; archive (do not delete) off-brand posts where the platform allows; update profile images, bios and highlights to match the palette |
| Publishing (light) | 3–4 posts: **"Fabric Close" series** of real ANKOO textile macro stills/clips. No logo except profile; no hard sell. |

### Phase 2 — The Moment: brand launch (Days 15–45)
**Goal:** launch the new direction with a flagship film.

| Week | Hero content | Supporting content |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Teaser: Threshold shot (6 s), Fabric Close (6 s) | Stills from the film, Stories countdown (quiet, no gimmicks) |
| 4 | **Brand film premiere**: YouTube (16:9 master) + Instagram/Facebook Reel + TikTok (9:16) + website hero loop | 30 s and 15 s cutdowns; WhatsApp Status segments; carousel "The Moment" stills |
| 5 | Founder reflection 01 (30–60 s, real voice): why ANKOO | Behind-the-scenes stills (real), Gathering still |
| 6 | Cutdown: *The Gathering* (15–30 s) | Detail series: sleeve, collar, cap |

### Phase 3 — The Collection and the Craft (Days 46–75)
**Goal:** show the product with editorial authority and prove the craftsmanship.

| Week | Hero content | Supporting content |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Collection film** (current/next collection, real garments) | Look films (10–20 s per look), 4:5 editorial carousels |
| 8 | **Craftsmanship film** (only real process footage) | "From Cloth to Moment" series: one stage per post |
| 9 | Founder reflection 02: on craft and fabric | Product presentation videos for WhatsApp sales conversations |
| 10 | Look films continued | Website product storytelling loops |

### Phase 4 — Culture and Experience (Days 76–90)
**Goal:** deepen meaning and show the complete ANKOO experience.

| Week | Hero content | Supporting content |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Cultural story: "E ja se Ankoo"** — the meaning of the name, unity through clothing (verified, EN + YO) | Carousel explainer; Yoruba Reel |
| 12 | **The ANKOO Experience**: packaging and presentation (real) | Unboxing still life; event content if genuine material exists |
| 13 | Review | Founder reflection 03 (optional); Day-90 review and next-quarter plan |

## 4. Weekly publishing rhythm (standard)

Quality over volume. If a piece fails QC, it does not publish; the slot stays empty.

| Platform | Weekly cadence |
|---|---|
| Instagram | 2 Reels · 1–2 feed posts (4:5 carousel or still) · Stories 3–5 days a week |
| TikTok | 2–3 videos (Reels adapted natively, not just reposted) |
| Facebook | 1–2 Reels/videos · 1 campaign post |
| YouTube | 1 long-form or film per phase · 1–2 Shorts per week |
| WhatsApp | Status 3–4 days a week · product videos sent one-to-one as needed |
| Website | Hero film updated at launch; collection film in Phase 3 |

### Grid rhythm (Instagram feed)
Repeat a three-beat rhythm so the grid reads as one editorial: **Detail · Silhouette · Portrait/Gathering**. Every post uses the ANKOO grade; the grid should look like one campaign from a distance.

## 6. Copy and caption voice

- Short, calm, confident. One or two lines.
- Let the image speak; captions add meaning, not hype.
- Yoruba where natural, written by fluent speakers.
- Calls to action are quiet: "Enquiries via WhatsApp." / "The collection is available now."
- No excessive emojis, no "🔥", no all-caps urgency, no "limited time only" pressure tactics.
- Hashtags: a small, consistent set (to be defined with the founder), not dozens.

## 7. Measurement

### Week 1 baseline
Record current followers, average reach, average views, average completion/watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, website visits and WhatsApp enquiries per week per platform. **Targets are set as improvements over this baseline after Week 1, not invented in advance.**

### Metrics that matter for a luxury brand
| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Average watch time / completion | Proves the film holds attention without loud tactics |
| Saves and shares | Signals aspiration and recommendation |
| Profile visits and follows from content | Brand pull |
| WhatsApp / DM enquiries | Commercial intent |
| Website sessions from social | Conversion path |
| Qualitative comments | Does the audience describe ANKOO as refined, premium, cultural? |

### Fortnightly review
Every two weeks: review metrics, comments and QC notes; adjust pacing, openings and cadence **within** the creative direction. The direction itself does not change based on short-term metrics.

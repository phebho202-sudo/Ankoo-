## Description

In 1911 the US Supreme Court broke Standard Oil into 34 companies. It was meant as a punishment, and it made John D. Rockefeller richer than ever. This is the full story: the bookkeeper from Cleveland, the secret railroad deals, the journalist who exposed them, the trial, the twist, and what it all says about power in any industry.

> Wonderful. We have consultations available at [confirm days/times] in [Akure / Lagos], or by video call. Which suits you best?

> Hello [Name], just checking in from ANKOO. If you're still planning your look for [occasion], we'd be glad to help whenever you're ready.

> Bespoke and personalised orders are made specifically for you, so they can't be returned. If there is ever a defect, our team will assess it and make it right.

## 11. Strongest recommendation: how ANKOO wins premium customers

**Sell the consultation, not the garment, and make the WhatsApp conversation as luxurious as the clothes.**

Premium bespoke buyers don't check out from an ad. They start a relationship. ANKOO's edge is the **experience**: the studio, the measuring, the fabric choice, the reveal. The paid funnel should therefore:

1. **Lead with real grooms and real moments** (the September wedding set is already proof of quality), framed around the date on his calendar: "Your wedding is in [month]. Let's begin."
2. **Make the reply the product.** Answer fast, ask good questions, use the owner's voice, and send a short, beautiful lookbook PDF in the chat. Many luxury brands lose the sale here, not in the ad.
3. **Shoot one proper product day** in the next 2–3 weeks: Agbada and Kaftan on 2–3 models, against forest green and ivory backdrops, plus 4–6 short vertical clips (measuring tape, fabric drape, embroidery close-up, the cap being placed, the final reveal at the mural). That single day removes the biggest creative gap and powers Campaigns A, B and D for months.
4. **Turn every delivered client into content (with consent).** Capture a fitting-day reel and a wedding-day photo, and ask for a genuine testimonial afterwards. These become retargeting assets that are real and verifiable.
5. **Treat the diaspora as phase two, done properly.** Remote measurement guide, video consultation, clear international timelines. Then advertise "ANKOO, from Akure to London" with confidence.

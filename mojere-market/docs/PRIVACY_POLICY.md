**Mojere Market Privacy Policy**

Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH IT]

Mojere Market ("we", "us") is a marketplace app where people in Ondo State, Nigeria list items and
contact each other to buy and sell. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we
share it with, and your rights.

**Who is responsible for your data**
[YOUR FULL NAME, or your registered business name], [YOUR ADDRESS, Ondo State, Nigeria].
Contact: phebho202@gmail.com

**1. What we collect**

*Information you give us*
- **Account:** your email address, password (stored only in scrambled/hashed form), display name,
  and the date you accepted our terms.
- **Profile:** optional bio and photo, and the area you choose (Local Government Area, town and
  neighbourhood). We do **not** use your phone's GPS.
- **Listings:** item titles, descriptions, prices, condition, category, area and photos.
- **Messages and offers:** messages you send to other users, offers, counteroffers and handover
  confirmations.
- **Reviews and reports:** ratings and comments you leave, and reports you send about listings,
  messages or people.
- **Business storefront (optional):** business name, description, logo, phone number, WhatsApp
  number and email that you choose to show.
- **Saved items, blocked users, and your notification settings.**

*Information created when you use the app*
- **Notifications** we create for you (for example "new message").
- **Push notification token:** if you allow notifications in an installed app, an identifier that
  lets us send alerts to your phone, and whether the phone is iPhone or Android.
- **Listing views:** we count how many times a listing is viewed. The count is not linked to who
  viewed it.
- **Payments (only if online payments are switched on):** the amount, purpose and status of a
  payment and the reference from our payment provider. We **never** see or store your card details.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and we do not
track you across other apps or websites.

**2. Why we use it (and the legal basis)**
- To create and run your account and show your listings, profile and storefront to other users
  (*performance of our contract with you*).
- To deliver messages, offers and notifications you ask for (*contract*).
- To keep the marketplace safe: reviewing reports, removing prohibited items, suspending accounts,
  and keeping an internal record of staff actions (*legitimate interests* and *legal obligations*).
- To send account emails, such as sign-up and password-reset codes (*contract*).
- To keep payment records for accounting and tax (*legal obligation*).
- Optional features such as push notifications use your *consent*, which you can withdraw at any
  time in your phone settings or in Profile → Notification settings.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

**3. What other users can see**
Other users can see your display name, general area, listings, storefront details you choose to
show, and reviews. They **cannot** see your email address unless you share it. Messages are visible
only to the people in the conversation. Our staff may read a reported message when they investigate
a report.

**4. Who we share data with**
We use trusted service providers, which process data only to run Mojere Market:
- **Supabase** stores our database, photos and login system. [CHECK the region of your Supabase
  project in Supabase → Project Settings and name it here, e.g. "servers in the European Union"].
- **Google (Gmail)** sends our account emails (sign-up and password-reset codes).
- **Expo** delivers push notifications to installed apps.
- **Paystack** processes payments, only if online payments are switched on.
- **Apple and Google** provide the app stores.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on safeguards allowed
under the NDPA. We may also disclose information when required by law, or to the police when
needed to investigate fraud or a crime.

**5. How long we keep it**
We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile,
login, listings, photos, messages, offers, reviews, saved items, notifications and storefront.
Payment records are kept for [6] years for accounting and legal reasons, without your name or
contact details. Our internal record that an account was deleted is kept for security.

**6. Your rights**
Under the NDPA you can ask to: access a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; object to or
restrict certain uses; withdraw consent; and receive your data in a portable format.
- **Delete your account yourself** in the app: Profile → Delete my account.
- **Edit your profile** in the app: Profile → Edit profile.
- For anything else, email phebho202@gmail.com. We will reply within [30] days.
You can also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**.

**7. Age**
Mojere Market is for people aged **[18]** and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

**8. Security**
We use encrypted connections, access rules that stop users from reading other people's private
data, and we never store card details. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong
password and never share your login codes.

**9. Changes**
If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is
important.
